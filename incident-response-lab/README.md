# Incident Response Lab

## Objective

The objective of this lab is to understand the basic steps
used to identify, investigate, document, and respond to
a cybersecurity incident.

## Incident Scenario

A user reports receiving a suspicious email containing
a link to an unknown website.

The security team must investigate the incident and
determine whether the user's account or computer may
have been compromised.

## Topics Covered

- Incident identification
- Evidence collection
- Incident documentation
- Containment
- Investigation
- Recovery
- Security monitoring

## Incident Response Process

### 1. Identification

Identify the suspicious activity and determine whether
it could represent a security incident.

### 2. Containment

Limit the potential impact by securing the affected
account or system.

### 3. Investigation

Review available information and evidence to understand
what happened and how the incident occurred.

### 4. Eradication

Remove the cause of the incident and address the
security weakness.

### 5. Recovery

Restore affected systems and accounts to normal operation
while monitoring for additional suspicious activity.

### 6. Documentation

Document the incident, actions taken, evidence collected,
and lessons learned.

## What I Learned

I learned that incident response requires a structured
process for identifying, investigating, containing,
and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

## Security Best Practices

- Report suspicious activity quickly
- Preserve evidence
- Document important events
- Follow incident response procedures
- Change compromised credentials
- Keep systems updated
- Monitor systems after an incident

## Conclusion

This lab helped me understand the basic incident response
process and the importance of documenting and responding
to cybersecurity incidents in a structured way.
